Class results

Load from the class Google Sheet

Enter the results key from setResultsKey in your Sheet’s Apps Script. The Sheet sends result codes only, never names.

Paste the column of result codes from your Google Forms response spreadsheet. Select the whole column in the linked Google Sheet, copy it, and paste it here. The header cell, timestamps, names, and blank cells are ignored, so a messy paste is fine. Codes collected any other way (a discussion board, an email, a typed list) work too.
